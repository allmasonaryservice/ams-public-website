## ABOUT US

All Masonry Services Inc is a professional masonry contractor with **nearly two decades of hands-on experience**. We specialize in brick installation, natural stone installation, custom home masonry, commercial masonry projects, and chimney construction.

### Our Story

My passion for masonry began at the age of 19 when I started working in the trade and learning from experienced masons. Coming from a masonry family, I also had the privilege of learning from my father, who owned and operated his own masonry company in the Chicagoland area for many years.

After years of working with two large masonry companies — gaining diverse techniques and skills — I founded **All Masonry Services Inc**. Today, with nearly 20 years of experience, my goal remains simple: deliver exceptional masonry work, build lasting client relationships, and treat every project as if it were my own.
